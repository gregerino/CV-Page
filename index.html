Course content within HR and payroll

Built myself, with AI

Recruitment OS

An AI tool that turns a requirements profile into a complete interview package: competency analysis, 15–20 structured questions with follow-ups, scorecards and bias-reduction guidance. It also holds a competency library with level indicators and Big Five personality competencies, so two managers hiring for the same role actually look for the same things.
